### Slide 1: Title & Tagline
- **Project Name:** AI Habit Insights (Productivity Track)
- **Tagline:** On-Device Intelligent Productivity & Procrastination Prevention for iQOO Phones
- **Team:** Member A, Member B, Member C

### Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Productivity apps require tedious manual logging and only show passive graphs.
- Users don't know *why* or *when* they procrastinate (cognitive fatigue, context mismatch, app switching).
- Cloud AI solutions raise serious privacy concerns for personal activity and work habits.

### Slide 3: Solution Overview
- A privacy-first, 100% on-device habit engine tailored for iQOO 15.
- Combines friction-free task management with background context sensing.
- Real-time intelligent diagnosis: identifies peak focus windows, procrastination patterns, and optimal work contexts.

### Slide 4: User Experience & UI Walkthrough (Member A)
- Clean, intuitive task launch and focus timer.
- Room DB SQLite local persistence with instant history filtering.
- Visual Habit Insight cards dynamically rendered from local analytics.

### Slide 5: On-Device Insight Engine Architecture (Member B)
- 100% local, on-device intelligence (zero cloud data leakage).
- Hourly completion clustering, task-type abandonment correlation, and environment classification.
- Explainable heuristic & statistical modeling, with roadmap for quantized on-device SLMs.

### Slide 6: Phone-First Hardware & Office Kit Integration (Member C)
- Leveraging iQOO 15 foreground context detection (`UsageStatsManager`).
- **Office Kit Bridge:** Seamlessly mirrors phone habit insights to laptop browser dashboard in real time.
- Low-latency, zero-battery-drain design.
